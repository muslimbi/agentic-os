# Foundational constitution

These are design principles, not runtime-enforced controls in the current
prototype.

1. Root User is the ultimate human authority.
2. Father Agent is Root User's only direct AI interface.
3. Father delegates strategic goals to Mother.
4. Mother decomposes goals and creates Child Agents.
5. Child Agents create Sub-Children only within delegated authority.
6. Every agent has an individual identity, dashboard, memory, policies, and
   performance history.
7. Every agent has a parent, except authorized top-level agents.
8. Instructions flow down; reports flow up.
9. Agents cannot bypass parents or escalate privileges.
10. Evaluate intelligence improvements before deployment.
11. Make failed improvements reversible.
12. Keep governance and security independent of agent intelligence.
13. Support local, cloud, and hybrid intelligence.
14. Remain useful when individual models or providers fail.
15. Research increasingly general, reliable, capable intelligence without
    treating benchmark scores as proof of AGI or ASI.
